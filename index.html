Common Questions

Good to Know

The things Waupaca asks us most. Can't find your answer? Call the front desk during staffed hours.

Where is Health and Fitness Headquarters located?
HFH is at N2610 Highway 22 in Waupaca, WI 54981, just off the main highway. We're a locally owned community gym serving Waupaca and the surrounding area. Stop in during staffed hours and we'll show you around, or call 715-256-1459 for directions.
What are your staffed hours?
Members have 24-hour access to HFH, so you can train on your own schedule. Staffed hours are Monday through Thursday 7:00am to 6:00pm, Friday 7:00am to 3:00pm, and Saturday 7:00am to noon. Sunday is unstaffed. Call 715-256-1459 during staffed hours about memberships or class sign-ups.
How much does a membership cost?
Membership rates aren't listed online. Call 715-256-1459 or stop in and we'll find the right fit. HFH offers flexible monthly memberships for individuals, couples and families, all with 24-hour member access, plus day passes, a weekly option and class drop-ins for visitors to the Waupaca area.
Do you offer day passes for visitors?
Yes. HFH offers day passes and a weekly option, which makes it straightforward if you're visiting the Waupaca area for a few days. Class drop-ins are available as well. Call 715-256-1459 or ask at the front desk for current day-pass and weekly rates.
What equipment does the gym have?
HFH has a full free-weight area with squat racks, benches and dumbbells, plate-loaded and selectorized strength machines, FreeMotion cable stations, a dedicated functional-training studio, and a cardio floor with treadmills, ellipticals and bikes. Locker rooms, a sauna and tanning are on site.
Do you have group fitness classes?
Yes. HFH runs group classes including Body Burn, HIRT, Fit Flow PiYo and Grit Strength, led by local instructors. Most run as six-week blocks with drop-in options. Days, times and pricing follow our current flyers, so call 715-256-1459 for this week's schedule.
Do you offer personal training?
Yes. Our trainers offer one-on-one personal training and small-group sessions tailored to your goals and experience level. If you prefer a class setting, we also run weekly group classes like HIRT with June. Ask at the front desk or send us a message to get started.
Is tanning available?
Yes. HFH has stand-up tanning booths and lay-down tanning beds on site, so you can keep your glow through the long Wisconsin winters. Tanning lotions and supplies are available right here. Ask the front desk about member rates and packages, or call 715-256-1459 for current pricing.

Still have a question? Call 715-256-1459 and talk to a real person.
